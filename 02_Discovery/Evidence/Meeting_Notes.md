# Stakeholder Interview

Interview ID: INT-001

Stakeholder
Chief Operating Officer

Date
July 2026

Interviewer
Jackson Biermann

---

## Objectives

Understand executive reporting pain points and desired future state.

---

## Key Findings

• Weekly reporting arrives too late to support Monday operational decisions.

• Data accuracy is generally acceptable, but validation consumes excessive analyst time.

• Executive dashboards should provide near real-time operational visibility.

• Reporting should require minimal manual intervention.

---

## Pain Points

- Delayed reporting
- Lack of data standardization
- Excessive analyst workload
- Limited KPI visibility

---

## Desired Outcomes

- Monday morning dashboard availability
- Single source of truth
- Automated reporting pipeline
- Improved executive confidence

---

## Business Value

High
