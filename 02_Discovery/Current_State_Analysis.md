# Current State Assessment

## Executive Summary

Analysis of the executive reporting workflow identified significant operational inefficiencies that delay the delivery of business insights and reduce analyst capacity. The current reporting process requires manual consolidation of data from six independent operational systems, resulting in approximately 80 labor hours each week before executive reporting can be completed.

These inefficiencies increase operational costs, introduce avoidable quality risks, and delay strategic decision-making across the organization.

This assessment focuses on the weekly Executive Operations Report generated by the Business Intelligence Department supporting Executive Leadership.

Included

- Data acquisition
- Data transformation
- Quality assurance
- Executive reporting
- Stakeholder communication

Excluded

- ERP modernization
- Customer reporting
- Financial forecasting
